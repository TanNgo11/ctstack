# Research: tanphatdigital.com (2026-09-27)

Vietnamese web/marketing agency (HCMC). Studied for layout and feature ideas only; don't copy text or visuals.

## Site map

Home · About · Services (+ ~14 service landing pages) · Projects (listing + case studies) · Tools (682 free tools) · Blog (~50 pages of posts) · Pricing calculator (`/cong-cu-tinh-gia-website`) · Contact · FAQ · Careers · Policies (warranty, privacy, terms, cookies).

## Global patterns

- Sticky header, 7 nav items. Floating contact buttons: phone, Zalo, Messenger.
- CTA band before the footer on every page ("Liên hệ ngay" + phone/Zalo/Facebook icons).
- Trust stats repeated: "10+ năm", "80+ dự án", "21 ngành", "100% hài lòng".
- Footer: logo + contact info, services column, quick links (FAQ, warranty, terms, privacy, cookies), 6 social links, newsletter.
- "24h" response promise everywhere.

## Page by page

## Their gaps (our chance to do better)

- Case studies without metrics, device mockups or before/after.
- Contact form without service/budget fields, map or working hours.
- Too broad an offering (blockchain, TikTok ads…) dilutes positioning.
- Blog has no authors (weak E-E-A-T).
